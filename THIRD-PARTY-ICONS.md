# Application identity marks

These vector paths identify the monitored applications. Brand artwork and trademarks belong to their respective owners. The project’s MIT license covers its original code.

- OpenAI knot in `openai.path` comes from the Codex desktop application.
- The DeepSeek whale in `deepseek.path` comes from the [DeepSeek chat favicon](https://fe-static.deepseek.com/chat/favicon.svg).
- The VS Code shape in `vscode.path` comes from the desktop application’s icon asset.
- The Antigravity silhouette in `antigravity.path` comes from [Google Antigravity press assets](https://antigravity.google/press).

The HUD scales these marks and applies source colors for legibility.
